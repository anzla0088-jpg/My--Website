10 Membership

HOW TO CUSTOMIZE
- Replace the logo text "IRONPULSE" in the header/footer of the HTML files.
- Replace phone, email and address in the footer.
- Replace the Unsplash image URLs with your client's photos if desired.
- Change colors in :root at the top of style.css.
- Change membership prices in pricing.html.
- Change class names/times in schedule.html.
- The forms are front-end demo forms; connect them to a backend/Formspree/EmailJS for real submissions.

Open index.html in a browser to start.
